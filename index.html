IntelliBrief

Relevant reports are spread across many outlets, repeat each other and arrive as isolated headlines. IntelliBrief collects licensed RSS, API and public data sources, removes duplicates and merges related reports into long-lived stories. Each story keeps its sources, its history and its connections to people, organisations, places and events.

  • Today view and briefings: personalised to your interests, location and reading time, in six languages.
  • StoryNet: a navigable map of how stories, entities and events relate.
  • Story timelines: what changed, what is new, and what is only repetition.
  • Attention signals: which developments are gaining public attention, and where.
  • Always traceable: original sources stay one tap away. AI helps select and summarise; it does not replace the evidence.

IntelliBrief will be available for iOS and Android.
