IntelliBrief

Relevant reports are spread across many outlets, repeat each other and arrive as isolated headlines. IntelliBrief collects licensed RSS, API and public data sources, removes duplicates and merges related reports into long-lived stories. Each story keeps its sources, its history and its connections to people, organisations, places and events.

  • Today: one finite page that answers “what changed for me”: stories matching the subjects, places and topics you follow, each saying why it is there, and what is coming up next.
  • Stories: the day’s biggest developments across every category, newest-first on request, with a narrated digest for each category.
  • Story timelines: how a story grew, what is new, and what is only repetition.
  • StoryNet: a navigable map of how stories, people, organisations and places connect.
  • Attention signals: which subjects the public is searching for right now, marked as interest, never as opinion.
  • Where things stand: official figures from central banks and statistics offices, and the economic calendar, beside the news.
  • Ask: source-checkable questions about current events, answered with the sources they rest on.
  • Six languages: English, German, French, Spanish, Italian and Persian.

How it works

  1. Collect. Licensed feeds, open APIs and official data, checked source by source before use.
  2. Connect. Duplicate reports are merged and related ones joined into stories that last for weeks.
  3. Personalise. Your follows, places and reading time decide what comes first, never a sponsor.
  4. Trace. Every summary links back to the original reporting, one tap away.

Principles

Sources first

AI helps select and summarise; it does not replace the evidence. Original sources stay visible on every story.

Licensed content only

Every source is reviewed for permitted use before it is collected. Sources that do not allow it are left out.

Honest signals

Coverage counts newsrooms, not articles. Public interest is shown as interest, and a quiet day is allowed to look quiet.

Funded by readers

IntelliBrief is planned as a subscription with monthly and annual plans, not as an advertising product. Prices will be announced at launch.
